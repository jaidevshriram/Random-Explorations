Bulldozer Part Inspector

The NeRF Lego bulldozer (train/r_19) rebuilt from LEGO pieces: each piece is one SVG group with its shading inside. Pieces come from SAM 2.1 masks plus prompts placed by eye. Hover to see what a region is, click to pin it, and use “By piece” to check the segmentation.

Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan, double-click to reset. Errors are RMSE in 0–255 levels over the pixels a part actually shows.

Semantic tree

object › piece › shade › part. Badges: sam automatic SAM mask, prompted mask from a prompt placed by eye, unsegmented pixels no piece covers. Kinds like “beam” on SAM pieces are guesses from colour and shape. Tick boxes hide or show a branch. RMSE is per visible pixel; share is that branch's part of the total squared error.

Hover a part to see where it sits in the hierarchy.
