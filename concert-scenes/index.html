Concert scenes

Two concert paintings rebuilt as animated canvas scenes. Each frame is drawn procedurally, run through an oil-paint shader, and can be recorded straight to video.

Arena light deskThe painted arena with a playable rig: turn the satellite in 3D, throw beams, gobos and lasers into the crowd, run the 30-second showcase. 1536×1024. Still StandingLandscape take on the single-beam painting: smoky cone, sketch-lit crowd, handwritten lyric. 1920×1080.
